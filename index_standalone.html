Artisanal Perfumery Born in the Heart of Lahore

Located in historical Main Ichra Bazar, Lahore, AR Perfumes was founded with a singular dedication: to craft unforgettable sensory signatures that evoke royal prestige and timeless elegance.

Every flacon is precision-compounded using aged Cambodian & Hindi Oud, rare Turkish roses, Tuscan leather notes, and crystal ambergris. Whether you seek non-alcoholic Attars for sacred devotion or modern Extraits de Parfum that command boardroom presence, AR Perfumes is your sanctuary of fine scent.
